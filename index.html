About Me

I'm a cybersecurity professional specializing as a SOC Analyst and Penetration Tester, with a strong foundation in web development. My passion lies at the intersection of offensive security and defensive operations — identifying threats before they escalate and hardening systems against real-world attacks.

On the SOC side, I monitor, detect, and respond to security incidents using SIEM tools, threat intelligence, and endpoint detection. As a pentester, I simulate adversarial techniques across networks, web applications, and cloud environments to uncover weaknesses that traditional scans often miss.

I also design and build secure web applications, applying the same security-first mindset I use in assessments. Whether writing detection rules, crafting exploits for authorized tests, or shipping clean front-end code, I focus on clarity, precision, and measurable impact.
